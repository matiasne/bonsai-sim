CARE GUIDE
  • 💧 WATER — keeps the soil moist. Trees drink more on hot, dry days. Shortcut: tap the open air above or beside the tree.
  • 🌫 MIST — leaves love humidity. Mist when the air is dry. Shortcut: tap the blossoms.
  • 🧪 FEED — fertilizer speeds up growth. Overdo it and the roots burn. Shortcut: tap the pebbles in the pot.
  • 🪵 BRANCH MENU — just tap any branch: a little ✂️/➰ menu appears right there.
  • ✂️ PRUNE — click a branch (or its blossom) to cut it. Pruned spots bud back denser.
  • 🍃 TRIM — pinch the blossom pads: trimmed pads regrow smaller and finer, ramification gets denser, and the opened crown catches light (a short growth boost). Costs a little vigor — a weak tree refuses.
  • ➰ WIRE — click a branch to wire it, then drag to bend; when you let go, the tool puts itself away. While the wire is on, just grab the copper coil and drag to re-bend anytime — no tool needed (a tap on the coil opens the menu to unwire). The wire must stay on 1–3 months to set — thin shoots are quickest, thick branches slowest, and the growing season helps — and removing it is always up to you (tap the coil → UNWIRE). Remove it after setting and the shape is kept; remove it early and the branch slowly springs back (you'll be warned first).
  • 🔮 FUTURE — preview your bonsai after 2 to 9 years of good care. Just a vision — nothing changes.
  • ⏩ — time-lapse: skip one hour of growth.
  • 〰️ ZEN SAND — the pot sits in a tray of sand. Drag across it to rake patterns (a tap presses a little dimple). Rain slowly smooths the sand.
  • 🌀 ROTATE — grab and drag the pot itself (or use ←/→) to see every side. Dragging the background does nothing.
  • 🖱 RIGHT-CLICK — puts any tool away, closes menus, cancels a drag (same as esc).
  • 🔍 ZOOM — scroll, pinch, +/− buttons, or the +/- keys. While zoomed in, drag up/down (or ↑/↓) to move the view; zooming out re-centers.

Your real sky drives the simulation: rain waters the tree, frost makes it dormant, wind makes it sway, night slows growth. The bonsai keeps living while the page is closed — come back and see what changed.
